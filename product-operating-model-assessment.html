The Modern Product Operating Model

Seven Stages. One Continuous Flow of Value.

High-performing product organizations run an integrated, end-to-end system: strategy sets direction, risk and research de-risk the investment, the portfolio funds the highest-value work, delivery ships it, adoption makes it real, and value review closes the loop. This assessment scores your organization against each stage's decision gate.
