The Problem

Green Status. Red Reality.

Critical programs rarely fail loudly. They drift: dependencies pile up, decisions queue behind governance that reviews instead of decides, and the plan becomes a schedule of activity instead of a path to outcomes. By the time the dashboard turns red, the program has usually been off track for months. These programs don't need more oversight. They need leadership that is accountable for the outcome, with the authority, cadence, and evidence to change course while course-change is still cheap.

How It Works

Diagnose. Stabilize. Drive. Transfer.

The same discipline every time, sized to the program. Designed, like everything we do, to make our role temporary.

01

Diagnose

A rapid, evidence-based read of the program: plan integrity, decision flow, risks, dependencies, and the honest distance between status and reality.

02

Stabilize

Reset the governance so it decides, rebuild the plan around outcomes, and put risks and dependencies under active management.

03

Drive

Lead execution with a weekly decision cadence, transparent reporting to the steering committee, and evidence over activity.

04

Transfer

Build your leaders into the roles we hold and hand over a program that runs on system, not personality.
