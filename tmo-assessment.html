The TMO Framework

Six Functions. One Managed System.

Most transformations fail in the space between strategy and delivery. This assessment measures how well your organization runs the six TMO functions: three that set direction (Strategic Leadership) and three that industrialize execution (Leverage at Scale).

Strategic Leadership

  • Vision, Strategy & Operating Models — one vision, one roadmap, one True North
  • Organizational Change & Communication — readiness, engagement, and communication as managed disciplines
  • Capability Development — build what the future state requires

Leverage at Scale

  • Program Management — orchestration against outcomes, not activity
  • Execution Services — playbooks, enablement, and metrics that make it repeatable
  • Staff Development — knowledge, adoption, and continuity that outlast the engagement

For each statement, move the slider from 0 — not at all to 10 — fully institutionalized and measured. Answer for how your transformation actually runs today, not how the charter says it should.
