Governance That Decides

Six Dimensions. One Test: Does Your Governance Decide?

Most governance tracks status; effective governance makes decisions. It knows who decides what, funds the highest-value work, surfaces risk at the right level, measures outcomes instead of activity, and gets lighter as trust in the system grows. This assessment scores your organization against each dimension's decision gate.

Direction & Investment

  • Decision Rights & Accountability — who decides what, decided once
  • Strategic Alignment — every investment traces to strategy
  • Funding & Prioritization — money follows value, not volume

Execution & Learning

  • Risk, Dependency & Escalation — issues resolved at the right level, fast
  • Performance & Value Realization — evidence over activity
  • Cadence & Adaptation — governance that learns and lightens

Answer each question on a 1–5 scale, from 1 — not happening to 5 — consistent, measured, and improving. Answer for how governance actually operates today, not how the operating model document says it should.
